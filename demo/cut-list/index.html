Build a list

Add pieces the way a job does, one at a time. The panel shows what the list costs at the saw as it grows. When it's done, send it to the yard.

What is running here

A best-fit-decreasing packer, in your browser. It re-solves the whole list on every piece you add, so the plan doesn't depend on the order you entered things. The engine at a yard uses Gilmore–Gomory column generation across every order in the queue at once, which is where the real yield comes from — this is the idea, not the product.

What the time figure claims

Cuts multiplied by minutes per cut, and nothing else. The rate is a parameter you set, not a number we're asserting. Hauling, staging and fastening aren't counted, because they happen either way. The saw is what leaves.
