What we're building

A cutting cell and the software that runs it, installed at the lumberyard. The yard takes a builder's approved cut list. The cell cuts every board to length, labels it, and bundles it by wall and floor.

Lumberyards buy the cell and sell pre-cut packages. Builders order through their yard, the way they buy lumber today.

Next, the same packages for anyone who wants to build something: no saw, no hours of measuring and cutting, no carpentry experience needed.

Less waste

Every cut is planned before the saw runs. Fewer boards bought, less in the dumpster.

Less time

Crews frame instead of measuring and cutting on site.

Safer sites

Less sawing on the jobsite means less time next to a running blade.

Precision

Every piece machine-cut to the length on the plan, every time.

Questions

What is Structmill building?

A machine and software that let a lumberyard cut a builder's framing to length, label every piece, and bundle it by wall and floor before it ships. The yard installs it. Builders get their framing pre-cut.

How does it help a builder?

Your framing shows up cut, labeled, and sorted by wall. You buy less lumber, throw away less, spend less time cutting, and run fewer saws on site. See it on a wall.

How does it help a lumberyard?

You sell finished framing packages instead of boards by the piece, match the pre-cut programs the chains offer without building a plant, and give your builders a reason to order every job from you. See the machine.

How much does it cost?

Lumberyards buy the cell outright or lease to own, with an annual software and maintenance package. Builders pay their lumberyard for pre-cut packages, at a price the yard sets. Planning with Structmill is free.
