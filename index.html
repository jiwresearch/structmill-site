Cut on site A stack of stock lengths and a saw. Every piece is measured, marked and cut in the weather.

What’s left over.

Less waste. No saw. Days back.

  • Optimized waste

    The whole list is solved at once, so one stock length carries several pieces instead of one piece and a remnant. What is left over stays at the yard, where it is still worth something.

  • No saw on site

    Pieces arrive at final length, labeled and in the order they go up. Nobody measures, nobody marks, and nobody makes the same cut for the thousandth time at three in the afternoon.

  • Time back

    Measuring and cutting is a real slice of every framing day, repeated thousands of times per house. Delivered pre-cut, the crew starts building the morning the truck shows up.
