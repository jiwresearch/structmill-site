Privacy

This covers the monthly updates signup on this site. Last updated October 19, 2026.

Your email address. If you choose to give them, your first name and which of the listed roles fits you best.

Only to send you Structmill updates. We use your name to address the email and your role to decide which updates are relevant to you.

Every update has an unsubscribe link. Click it and you won't get any more updates.
